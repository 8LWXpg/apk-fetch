    About YouTube Music

    Reviewed byBernadine WisokyContent Editor

    YouTube Music is a music streaming app that connects you to songs, videos, and podcasts.

    YouTube Music helps when you want one place for music and audio shows that fits your day. It brings a large catalog together and adjusts recommendations as your tastes change. Whether you need background sounds for work or a playlist for a drive, the app organizes options in a way that is easy to browse. For users looking to install outside standard channels, the YouTube Music APK offers the same core experience within a familiar interface.


    Features

    Vast library of music, videos, and podcasts

    YouTube Music offers access to over 100 million official tracks along with music videos. You can also find live performances, covers, and remixes that are not always available in other places. In addition, podcasts sit alongside music, so you can switch from songs to episodes without changing apps.

    Personal mixes and easy discovery

    The app creates made-for-you mixes and activity mixes for workouts, relaxing, and focus. Discovery is supported through the Explore and Samples tabs, plus genre and mood hubs and global charts. When you want something specific yet flexible, YouTube Music's Radio Builder lets you start a station from chosen artists or songs, then adjust mood and variety with simple filters.

    Playback built for switching and singing

    It lets you move from audio to video with one tap during playback. Synced lyrics help you follow lines as they play, which can support both sing-along moments and focused listening. This setup makes it easy to stay on the same track while changing how you listen.

    Works across devices and contexts

    YouTube Music works on phones, desktops, TVs, speakers, cars, and Wear OS watches, so your music follows you. A Wear OS Tile provides quick controls on your wrist. Integrations with Google Assistant, Maps, and Waze allow voice control and in-route playback, which helps keep your hands free while driving.

    Music Premium benefits

    With Music Premium, YouTube Music adds ad-free listening, background play when the screen is off, and offline downloads. Smart downloads can refresh selections automatically so you have music ready when you are offline. The free tier includes ads and does not support background audio or offline downloads. If you have YouTube Premium, you receive all Music Premium benefits inside the app.


    Before you download YouTube Music APK

    Account tiers affect core functions. Background play and offline downloads work with Music Premium and are not available on the free tier. YouTube Premium includes the full set of Music Premium benefits inside YouTube Music, which can be helpful if you already use other YouTube services. If you plan to listen offline, note that downloads are managed within the app and do not export as MP3 or MP4 files.


    Offline downloads and file handling

    Downloads created in YouTube Music are designed for playback inside the app. Files are not visible as standard audio or video files in your device storage. Smart downloads can update content when connected, so recent tracks are ready before you go offline. For the best stability and features, where available, consider downloading YouTube Music latest version from APKPure.


    How to use YouTube Music on Android

    The app starts quickly, then guides you toward mixes and stations based on your listening. For the best performance and updated tools like synced lyrics and discovery tabs, use the YouTube Music latest version. The steps below outline a simple path from first launch to everyday use.

    1. Open YouTube Music and sign in with your account to sync likes, history, and subscriptions.

    2. Browse Home, Explore, or Samples to find mixes by mood or genre, then tap a card to start playing.

    3. Use Search to find a song or artist, then create a radio station with Radio Builder and set mood and variety.

    4. Add tracks to your Library or playlists, and create a collaborative playlist if you plan to build a set with friends.

    5. During playback, tap the video button to switch to video, turn on lyrics if available, and if you have Music Premium, enable downloads or smart downloads for offline listening. Use Assistant or Maps integration for voice control while driving.


    Start listening with YouTube Music

    YouTube Music brings official tracks, videos, live sessions, and podcasts into one place with flexible discovery and playback. To begin, download YouTube Music and follow the steps above to set up your library and stations.
