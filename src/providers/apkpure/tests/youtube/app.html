If you record on your phone, you can upload or go live from the same screen. For the smoothest experience, consider updating to the YouTube latest version.     FAQs

    How to download YouTube videos?

    To download YouTube videos for offline viewing, you need a YouTube Premium subscription, which allows you to tap the download button under any video and save it directly within the official app without using third-party tools like youtube downloader or youtube video downloader.

    How to cancel YouTube TV?

    To cancel youtube tv, simply sign in to tv.youtube.com on your browser, go to Settings, then Membership, and click Cancel (or Pause) to stop your subscription without needing to call customer support.

    Is YouTube TV dropping Fox News?

    As of now, youtube tv has not announced any plans to drop Fox News, and the channel remains available in the base package, though carriage agreements can change so it is best to check YouTube TV's official channel lineup for the latest updates.

    How much does YouTube pay per view?

    YouTube does not pay a fixed amount per view; instead, most creators earn through AdSense revenue that typically ranges from 0.001to0.001to0.003 per view (roughly 1to1to3 per 1,000 views), though actual earnings vary based on factors like ad type, viewer location, video length, and audience engagement.

    How to turn off Restricted Mode on YouTube?

    To turn off Restricted Mode on YouTube, click your profile picture, select Restricted Mode (or go to Settings then General on mobile), and toggle it off, though you may need to sign in again if your account or network administrator enforces this setting.
