    About NewPipe

    Reviewed byBernadine WisokyContent Editor

    NewPipe is a video player app that lets you watch YouTube without ads.

    NewPipe suits users who want a clean video experience without tracking or interruptions. It focuses on fast access to content and a simple layout that feels familiar, so you can search and browse with ease. The app works without Google services, which can help protect your data and run smoothly on modest devices. It is handy for commutes, study breaks, or quiet listening at night. To try it on your phone or tablet, download NewPipe and follow the in app prompts.


    NewPipe Features

    Watch without ads

    NewPipe removes video advertisements so playback starts immediately. You can open a video, explore related items, and keep watching without interruptions.

    Save videos and audio for offline use

    You can download videos for offline viewing and choose the quality that fits your storage and data plan. Pick from 144p up to 1080p, or save audio only when you just need the sound.

    Background and popup playback

    Keep audio playing while using other apps or when the screen is locked. You can also open a small popup window that floats over other apps, which helps when chatting, browsing, or taking notes.

    Privacy and library control

    NewPipe does not use Google APIs or frameworks and aims to avoid tracking. You can subscribe to channels manually, create playlists, mark favorites, and export your subscriptions. The project is open source, so its code can be reviewed by the community.


    Get started with NewPipe

    NewPipe delivers ad free viewing, offline saving, and flexible playback with a privacy first approach. To begin, install the NewPipe APK on your device and open the settings to set your default quality and download folders. Adjust playback options to match your data and battery needs before you start watching.
